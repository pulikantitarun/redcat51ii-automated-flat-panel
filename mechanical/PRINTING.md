# Printing settings

Use PETG, ASA or ABS; do not use PLA for an enclosure or clamp left in sun. Recommended: 0.20 mm layer, four perimeters, five top/bottom layers, 35-45% gyroid infill. Use 60% infill for the clamp and tray arm. Print the fit gauge first. Print two copies of part 08.

The electronics base is matched to the Rev B 120 x 75 mm PCB and its four asymmetric mounting holes. Do not reuse the Revision A enclosure. Install M3 screws into the printed standoffs gently; heat-set inserts may be added if the enclosure will be opened repeatedly.
